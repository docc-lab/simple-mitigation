\begin{figure}[t]

  \caption{Harvesting (matched) mitigates bursts; replication (mismatched) fails. Under 10\,\% co-tenant bursts,
    harvesting keeps the victim's p95 within the SLO (78\,$\mu$s) on its own two cores, even lending 0.4 of them to the
    aggressors; replication allocates up to six more cores and still misses it (93\,$\mu$s), barely better than no
    mitigation (106\,$\mu$s). Each box sits at that configuration's p95 latency during the bursts (dot: median; bar:
    min--max over 2 trials) and shows the victim's two allocated cores, filled with CPU time used by the victim
    (purple) or by replicas (orange; they run on their own cores and are stacked here for comparison), lent to the
    aggressors (blue), or idle (gray). Dotted: the victim's p95 outside the bursts (70\,$\mu$s); the SLO is
    1.3$\times$ that (91\,$\mu$s).}
  \label{fig:motiv-tower}
